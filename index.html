5Agreement and signature

By signing this form, I agree to using the Stafford Emergency Foodbank and will move on to the Community Social Supermarket project when my crisis is over. I agree to engage with the project leaders for help and advice, so they can help me support my family and improve my situation. The rules are:

  1. In an emergency I will be issued with 1 single person or family food hamper, which will include evening meals, vegetables, fruit and other items needed for a week.
  2. On the second visit, I agree to accept support from the organisers to help me change the circumstances behind my foodbank use, and work out how long I might need it for.
  3. If a third visit is needed, a further plan will be put in place with an experienced local organiser at the Community Social Supermarket. A route to a personal solution will be explored, and other local services will be looked at, such as signposting to Citizens Advice.
  4. We will only issue 6 foodbanks in 6 months unless there are extenuating circumstances.
